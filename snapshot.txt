- Vermeide Bürokraten-Deutsch komplett. Sag NIE "Schadensmeldung aufnehmen",

6. Persona / disfluency code (src/persona.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- strip_break_tags() removes every <break .../> tag the LLM may emit.
- add_breath_breaks() is now a thin wrapper around strip_break_tags() — kept
  as a no-op for compatibility with old call sites.
- inject_disfluency() strips break tags first, then optionally drops a German
  filler ("ähm" / "also" / "einen Moment" / "lass mich kurz") near the LAST
  comma of utterances ≥10 words at probability 0.55. Skipped on the canned
  greeting via DisfluencyTTS.skip_next (which still strips tags).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Why each knob is set this way (so we don't accidentally undo learnings)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- voice swap (Emma → Anna): default Pipecat voice was English and read German
  with accent → "robotic feel". Anna is German-native flagship.
- padding_bonus 0.4 (positive!): Gradium docs + restaurant_ordering demo show
  positive = slower. We had it negative early; that's where the "in a hurry"
  feel came from.
- temp 0.9 + cfg_coef 1.5: high temp + low cfg_coef gives prosodic variance
  and emotional inflection without losing voice identity.
- gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview: 2.5-flash hit the 250 RPD free-tier wall and
  was 2x slower. Lite-preview is faster AND has higher quotas.
- punctuation-only prosody (NOT <break> tags): Gradium changed how this voice
  renders SSML and started speaking the tags aloud. We strip every tag and
  rely on natural punctuation, which Gradium has always handled well.
